EMBERHUSH — Three Quiet Heists. A top-down stealth game: slip past patrolling guards, read their raycast vision cones, hide in ferns and shadow, lure guards away with pebbles, take every relic and reach the green exit unseen. WASD / arrows move, Shift sneaks, click or Space throws a pebble. Three heists, five guards, six relics — all art and sound made for this game, no files loaded from the network.
